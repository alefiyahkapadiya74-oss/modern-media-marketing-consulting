# ROADMAP: Modern Media & Marketing Consulting FZC

## Overview

Phased development roadmap for building the luxury, Awwwards-inspired digital platform for Modern Media & Marketing Consulting FZC in Sharjah Publishing City Free Zone, UAE.

- **Phase 1: Foundation & Core Design System** — Next.js project setup, luxury Tailwind color system (#234038 Forest Green, #B88A44 Brass Gold, #F7F4EC Warm Ivory), Playfair Display & Inter typography, and Lenis smooth scroll provider integration.
- **Phase 2: Header Navigation & Cinematic Hero Section** — Dynamic transparent header, sticky navigation state, slide-over mobile menu, and large editorial Playfair hero with GSAP text entrance animations.
- **Phase 3: Agency Narrative (About & Services Suite)** — Editorial company background, Mission/Vision/Values, Why Choose Us, and 7-service interactive consultancy cards.
- **Phase 4: Process Walkthrough, Portfolio & Case Studies** — Interactive 6-step workflow, Bento/Masonry portfolio gallery, image hover/stacking effects, and detailed client case studies.
- **Phase 5: Testimonials, Contact & Google Maps** — High-profile client testimonials slider, functional B2B contact form, Sharjah Publishing City Free Zone contact details, and custom Google Maps embed.
- **Phase 6: GSAP ScrollTrigger Motion Polish & Performance Tuning** — Comprehensive Lenis inertia tuning, ScrollTrigger pinning/parallax, micro-interactions, responsive testing, and Awwwards-level visual polish.

## Phase Details

### Phase 1: Foundation & Core Design System
**Goal:** Establish the technical foundation, luxury design token system, custom typography, and global smooth scroll provider.
**Mode:** mvp
**UI hint:** yes
**Requirements:** SYS-01, SYS-02, SYS-03
**Success Criteria:**
1. Next.js 14+ project initialized with TypeScript and Tailwind CSS configuring Deep Forest Green (`#234038`), Brass Gold (`#B88A44`), and Warm Ivory (`#F7F4EC`).
2. Google Fonts (`Playfair Display` and `Inter`) loaded and verified across headings and body copy.
3. Lenis smooth scroll provider wrapper configured and active in root layout.

### Phase 2: Header Navigation & Cinematic Hero Section
**Goal:** Build the dynamic transparent navigation header and the Awwwards-inspired cinematic hero section.
**Mode:** mvp
**UI hint:** yes
**Requirements:** NAV-01, NAV-02, NAV-03, HERO-01, HERO-02, HERO-03
