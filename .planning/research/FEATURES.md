# Project Research: Features & UI Architecture

## Table Stakes Features
- **Hero Section**: Large cinematic hero with immersive editorial layout, Playfair Display typography, and CTA controls.
- **Transparent & Sticky Navbar**: Dynamic navigation header with blur backdrop effect and smooth scroll-to-section links.
- **About Section**: Storytelling layout presenting Company Mission, Vision, Values, and Why Choose Us.
- **Services Directory**: Comprehensive grid showcasing 7 core service offerings (Marketing, Media Buying, Brand Strategy, Web Dev, Social Media, Design, HR).
- **Process Showcase**: Interactive 6-step workflow visualization (Discovery -> Research -> Strategy -> Execution -> Optimization -> Growth).
- **Portfolio & Case Studies**: Bento grid & masonry galleries showcasing luxury client projects and measurable success stories.
- **Testimonials Slider/Grid**: Client feedback rendered with high-end typography and card elevation.
- **Contact & Map**: Functional contact form, official address in Sharjah Publishing City Free Zone, interactive Google Map embed, direct email/phone.

## Differentiating Features (Awwwards-Level Polish)
- **Lenis Smooth Scroll & Physics-based Inertia**.
- **GSAP Scroll-Pinning & Text Reveal Animations**.
- **Horizontal Scroll Gallery for Portfolio Highlights**.
- **Interactive Micro-Interactions** (Custom cursor option, card tilt, hover scale effects).
- **Dark Forest Green & Brass Gold Modern Aesthetic**.
