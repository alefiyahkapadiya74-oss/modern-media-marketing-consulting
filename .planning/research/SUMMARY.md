# Project Research Summary

## Key Findings

### Stack Recommendation
- **Next.js 14+ (App Router)** with **TypeScript** & **Tailwind CSS**.
- **Lenis** for smooth scroll inertia.
- **GSAP** & **ScrollTrigger** for pinning, horizontal scroll, and scroll-driven motion.
- **Framer Motion** for UI gestures and micro-interactions.
- **Playfair Display** & **Inter** via `next/font`.

### Key Capabilities
- Cinematic Hero with text reveal & Playfair Display typography.
- Transparent & Sticky Navbar.
- Editorial About Section (Story, Mission, Vision, Why Choose Us).
- 7 Service Showcases & 6-Step Process Walkthrough.
- Bento & Masonry Portfolio + Detailed Case Studies.
- Testimonials & Contact Section with Google Maps embed and inquiry form.

### Implications for Roadmap
- Phase 1: Architecture, Design Tokens & Smooth Scroll Provider.
- Phase 2: Navigation & Hero Narrative Section.
- Phase 3: Agency Story (About & Services Showcase).
- Phase 4: Process Walkthrough & Portfolio / Case Studies.
- Phase 5: Testimonials, Contact Form & Google Maps.
- Phase 6: Motion Refinement, Awwwards Polish & Responsive Testing.
